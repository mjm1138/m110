M110

Complete the catalog.

A photo library for your smart telescope. A desktop app that turns a growing pile of Seestar or DwarfLab captures into an organized deep-sky library — catalog, capture tracking, ingest, Siril processing-prep, session planning, a sky map of everything you've shot, and safe backups, with a north-star goal to chase.

Download for macOS · Linux · Windows Read the user guide

✨ M110 is free and open source — and always will be. No subscription, no licence key. Apache-2.0 licensed, built in the open, and yours to keep.

Everything your captures deserve

🗂️

A real library

Every object you've shot, organized by catalog (Messier, Caldwell, and more) with status, integration time, and a per-object journal.

🗺️

Your sky, mapped

Plot your whole collection on a star chart. Filter to a goal and the rest of that list joins the chart in grey — so you can see your progress, and the gaps.

📥

One-click ingest

Point it at your Seestar, DwarfLab scope, or a folder; it groups, names, and files your subs, stacks, and finished renders — you preview before anything is written.

🔭

Capture tracking

Sessions, frames, filters, and integration roll up automatically from your FITS headers, so you always know what's done and what needs another night.

✨

Siril processing-prep

Automatically arranges a clean, contained Siril sandbox per target — lights linked, a tuned preset ready — then imports your finished work back.

🎯

A goal to chase

Track your progress through a curated catalog (Messier, Caldwell, and more) or your own custom goals — always knowing what's still missing. Named for the 110th and final Messier object.

🌙

Plan your nights

See what's up tonight, ranked by what your goals need — with a moon-aware schedule, an altitude timeline, and a printable field guide for the session.

💬

Ask your library

Connect your own AI assistant and ask it things like "what should I shoot tonight?" or "critique my M51" — answered from your site, horizon, and capture history. It can suggest; it can't change your library.

💾

Safe by design

Local-first, open source, and careful with your data — with built-in, hardlinked backups so an irreplaceable library stays that way.
