## BUG 1 — Moon separation column is wrong (2× off)

**Observed:** M3 row shows `101° · none`.
**Ground truth:** moon–M3 great-circle separation at the slot start (22:30
MDT) is **45°**.

The impact verdict "none" is *accidentally* correct — the 17%-lit moon is at
~1° altitude and sets at 22:35, five minutes into the slot — but the printed
separation is more than 2× the real value. 101° doesn't match the obvious
failure modes (raw ΔRA ≈ 50°; ΔRA·cos δ smaller still), which makes it worth
finding: candidates are the moon's position evaluated at some *other* time
(e.g. a fixed local-midnight moon vs. slot-start target), a degrees/radians
slip partially masked, or separation measured from the anti-lunar point.

The `—` (moon below horizon) rendering for the other three rows is correct.

**Suggested fix shape:** compute separation topocentrically at the slot's
start time, same instant for both bodies; unit-test against a couple of
known ephemeris values (e.g. this exact case: 2026-07-17 22:30 MDT, M3 at
RA 205.548° Dec 28.377°, expect ≈45°).

## GAP 1 — Planner abandons the back half of the night

The plan ends at 01:00; astronomical dark runs to ~03:50. Nearly three hours
of dark, on a 17%-moon night with goal=Messier, are left unscheduled while
untouched-in-store Messier targets sit at good altitude after 01:00: M27
(65–73°), M15 and M2 (rising through 40–60°), M39, M29, M14, M11.

The urgency-driven front half (closing-window spring objects first) is good
planning. After the urgent set is exhausted, the planner should fill the
remaining dark with the best non-urgent candidates (highest-value observable
targets by the same scoring) rather than stopping.

## GAP 2 — No minimum-useful-slot / marginal-data guard

M63 and M51 get 20-minute token slots at 34–36° altitude, *descending* in the
NW. At that airmass, frame-rejection at stack time runs well above the ~30%
baseline (real-world S50 experience: low-altitude, descending targets reject
heavily on FWHM/roundness), so the usable yield of a 20-minute slot may be
10 minutes or less. Legitimate as deliberate "last chance before the window
closes" grabs, but the planner should either (a) enforce a configurable
minimum slot duration after expected rejection, or (b) explicitly annotate
such slots as marginal/last-chance in the plan output so the human can drop
them knowingly.

## NOTE 1 — Footer stamps the plan date, not the generation date

Plan footer reads `Generated by M110 · 2026-07-17`, but the store's
`prioritized.json` records `generated: 2026-07-14` (the actual harness run
date). The footer should carry the generation timestamp (or both: "generated
2026-07-14 for 2026-07-17").

## NOTE 2 — Decide which source of truth outranks: hand priorities vs. store integration

The fixture `priorities.toml` marks M3 `priority = "low"`, strategy "Target
met May 14 — opportunistic only", yet the planner gave it the night's longest
slot (70 min). That's *consistent* with ranking purely from the store's own
integration data (M3 = 0 min captured in the fixture store — untouched), and
ignoring the hand list's demotion. If `priorities.toml` is legacy input in
M110's model, fine — but make that explicit (and consider surfacing "hand list
disagrees" as a lint), so a user who hand-demotes a target isn't surprised
when the planner promotes it from its own bookkeeping.

# Resolutions (2026-07-14, `feature/session-planner`)

**BUG 1 — FIXED.** Root cause confirmed as the astropy frame pitfall:
`icrs_target.separation(gcrs_moon)` re-expresses the moon's direction from the
**barycenter** (astropy even emits `NonRotationTransformationWarning`), giving
100.9° where the true topocentric separation is 45.2° — the exact 101/45 pair
from the review. `night_track` now transforms both bodies into a **common
topocentric AltAz frame** and evaluates at the proposed **start** (not transit).
Regression test pins the review's ground-truth case (M3, 2026-07-17, ≈45°).

**GAP 1 — FIXED.** `sequence_plan(fill=True)` (default): after the `count`
primary slots, the sequencer keeps scheduling until dawn or candidates run out —
`count` sizes the slots (night ÷ count), it no longer truncates the night. The
review night now schedules 6 targets to 03:50 instead of 4 to 01:00.
`fill=False` restores the strict cut-off (and the UI's manual reorder reflow
stays bounded to the user's list).

**GAP 2 — FIXED (option b: annotate).** A slot ≤ `MARGINAL_SLOT_MIN` (30 min)
truncated by the target's **own closing window** while it **descends** is
flagged `marginal` and rendered **⚠** (field guide footnote + UI tooltip:
"last-chance slot … expect heavy frame rejection; keep or drop knowingly").
Deep-stack-capped and dawn-capped short slots are not flagged (the target
isn't sinking away).

**NOTE 1 — FIXED.** Footer now stamps both:
`Generated by M110 · <today> · for the night of <plan date>`.

**NOTE 2 — ANSWERED (no code change).** `priorities.toml` is **retired** as of
PLANNING_ROADMAP Phase 1.1 / BUGS #35: nothing in the ranking path reads it
(the fixture store predates the retirement). Manual intent lives in **pins**
(pin/deprioritize) + the strategy/weights knobs; the "hand list disagrees" lint
is moot because there is no hand list anymore.
